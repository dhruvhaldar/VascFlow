## 2024-05-24 - Avoid Dead-end Interactions in Exhaustible Forms
**Learning:** Leaving a finite-option form visible when all its options are exhausted (e.g., all mesh faces have boundary conditions assigned) creates a confusing and dead-end interaction for the user. Simply disabling the inputs is insufficient and feels broken.
**Action:** Conditionally hide the form and replace it with an explicit instructional or success state message when options are exhausted. Crucially, if the active element is removed from the DOM, programmatically shift keyboard focus to the new message container to maintain accessibility and navigation flow.
